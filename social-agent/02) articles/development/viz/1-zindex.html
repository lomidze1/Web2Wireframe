z-index: 9999 doesn't always win.

z-index only orders elements inside their own stacking context.
.parent { transform: translateZ(0) }
z-index: 9999
child of .parent
z-index: 2
sibling of .parent
↑ sealed inside — the number can't reach out
The dark card wins.
One transform on the ancestor created a new context. Everything inside is compared only against itself.

Nothing about the element explains the result.
