## Description (სრული)

```
Choose the output before you import. Real, editable Figma layers from any URL,
screenshot or HTML — website-faithful or your own wireframe style, your call.

Import from a live URL, a screenshot, or raw HTML. Pages that sit behind a login
can be captured with the free Web2Wireframe browser extension. What lands on your
canvas is organized frames with editable text, colors, fonts, images and vectors —
not a flat picture.

Set per import:
• Fidelity — the site's own colors and web fonts, or your own wireframe palette and font
• Text — the page's real copy, readable placeholders, or lorem ipsum
• Assets — real images and icons, or placeholders
• Layout — auto layout, or absolute positioning
• Viewports — up to 3 in one run (desktop, tablet, mobile)
• Hover states — imported as component variants
• CSS variables — as Figma styles, or as plain values
• Layer names — readable names (Header, Link – Sign in), or the original HTML tags
• Links — keep the page's links, or import without them

What people use it for:
• Teardowns and competitive research
• Redesigns — start from the live page, not a screenshot
• Component mining — pull a real nav, card or footer into your own system
• Design reviews and audits, with the real page next to your version
• Wireframes — turn a live page into a lo-fi frame in your own palette
• Client and stakeholder presentations

Free plan: 10 imports a month, full fidelity. Pro: unlimited imports.
Support: support@web2wireframe.design
```
